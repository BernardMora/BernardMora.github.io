10:T1610,<body><h1>Why We Need to Talk More About Tech and Education</h1><p><em>—especially when it comes to understanding modern technology and how it shapes our lives.</em></p><p>Over the past few decades, we’ve seen incredible technological breakthroughs. Think back to the birth of the internet in the '80s and '90s. It revolutionized the way we access information and communicate. Then came social media—a whole new world where people across the globe could connect instantly and share ideas in real time.</p><p>These platforms have become deeply embedded in our daily routines—so much so that we often don’t even notice how much time we spend on them or how they influence our behaviors and decisions. And now, artificial intelligence has joined the mix. Just like the internet and social media before it, AI is quietly, yet powerfully, reshaping the way we live—whether we realize it or not.</p><p>While these technologies bring undeniable benefits, they also introduce serious risks. And unfortunately, those risks often go unnoticed—especially by people who aren’t familiar with how the technology works or the increasingly complex and sometimes dangerous ways it can be used.</p><h2>Deepfakes and Digital Scams</h2><p>Take <a href='https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deepfake'>deepfakes</a>, for example. There are real-world cases of people being scammed through fake voices and images . Often, victims had no idea this kind of technology even existed—let alone how to protect themselves from it.</p><img src='/img/blog/education-in-technology/1.png' alt='Martin Lewis deepfake example' style='max-width: 100%; height: auto;'><blockquote><p><strong>Martin Lewis</strong>, English financial journalist and broadcaster, is deepfaked into promoting a fictitious company called <em>Quantum AI</em>, which is a scam. <a href='https://synthedia.substack.com/p/deepfake-scam-video-features-british'>Source</a></p></blockquote><h2>The Broader Impact of Technology</h2><p>And the issue isn’t just about what the technology can do—it's also about how we use it, and how that usage impacts our daily lives.</p><h3>Social Media and Attention</h3><p>Let’s take social media. Studies show that excessive use can negatively affect nearly every area of life: work, relationships, and mental health.</p><p><strong>Why is that?</strong></p><p>Because it affects our attention. Research shows that higher social media use is consistently linked with poor attentional control and increased distractibility in teenagers and young adults. The ripple effects of reduced focus and constant distraction can be felt in every corner of daily life.</p><p><a href='https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4872178'>Technology and Digital Media's Impact on Attention Span in Teenagers and Young Adults</a></p><h3>Sleep and Executive Function</h3><p>It also affects our sleep. One study found that social media addiction is associated with impaired executive functioning, emotional disturbance, and poor sleep quality.</p><p><a href='https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10226546/'>Effect of Social Media Addiction on Executive Functioning Among Young Adults</a></p><h3>Link to Harmful Behaviors</h3><p>And it’s linked to harmful behaviors. Excessive use among high school students has been associated with increased risks of smoking, depression, anxiety, stress, suicidal thoughts, and drug use.</p><p><a href='https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9150901/'>Excessive use of social media by high school students in southern Brazil</a></p><h2>The Positive Side of Technology</h2><p>Of course, it’s not all bad. Technology has brought incredible value to our lives as well:</p><ul><li><strong>Access to Information and Learning:</strong> Platforms like YouTube and LinkedIn Learning offer tutorials, lectures, and educational resources on virtually every subject imaginable.</li><li><strong>Better Communication:</strong> Social media enables real-time, barrier-free interaction—whether in classrooms, businesses, or personal relationships.</li><li><strong>Business and Industry Optimization:</strong> In fields like finance, retail, and manufacturing, AI streamlines workflows, boosts decision-making, and enhances customer experiences—driving productivity and innovation.</li></ul><h2>Why Tech Education Is Essential</h2><p>That’s why I believe education about technology isn’t just important—it’s essential.</p><p>Some might argue that as people become more familiar with technology, its negative effects fade. And while there may be some truth to that, it doesn’t mean we can ignore the need for foundational education—especially for those who are least familiar with it, like children or individuals growing up in an increasingly tech-saturated world.</p><p>In my own country, I see friends and people around me using these tools without ever questioning their impact. They’re simply going with the flow, unaware of how this technology may be shaping their thoughts, habits, and lives—for better or worse.</p><p>And that’s the gap we need to bridge. Some people have learned how to use technology as a powerful tool—and they are the ones who benefit most from it. But for the vast majority, that knowledge is still missing. They’re being shaped by the tool instead of shaping how they use it.</p><div style='border: 2px solid #ccc; padding: 20px; margin-top: 30px; background-color: #f9f9f9;'><h3>My takeaway:</h3><p>Technology is an incredible ally in achieving your goals—if you have the right knowledge to use it wisely.
